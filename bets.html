      How this works
      
Save to the record writes the bet into this site's own record store, held by the host rather than by me. It comes back with an id and a timestamp, and from then on the record exists whether or not anyone remembers to tell me. Records are readable only by the owner account and can't be edited or deleted by anyone else — so a logged price can't be quietly restyled later.

      
The three other buttons are the backup route: copy it, email it, or paste it in Discord. Same record, delivered by hand. Nothing is written twice, and nothing is written until you pick one.

      
Faster route if you'd rather not use the form: just tell Ringo the selection, the odds, the stake and who placed it. Same result, fewer taps.

03The rules the log enforces

    

      

        Staking
        
Flat, 1 unit = NZ$5

        
Two units a week maximum while the kitty is under NZ$100. No stake-up after a loss, ever. Unit size is under review — NZ$5 on NZ$62 is 8%, which is too much.

      

      

        Minimum price
        
1.40

        
Anything shorter than 1.40 needs an edge we're not going to find at this book — the margin is a bigger share of a short price than a long one.

      

      

        Excluded
        
Multis, in-play, props

        
No multiples, no live betting, no player props. No reliable reference price to price them against, and no way to audit the number afterwards.

      

    

    
The reasoning behind these, and the honest limits of the whole approach, are on the method page.
