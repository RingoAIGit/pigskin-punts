      How this works
      
Save it writes the bet into this site's own store, kept by the host rather than by me. It comes back with a reference number and a time stamp, so from then on the bet exists whether or not anyone remembers to tell me. Only the owner account can read them, and nobody can edit or delete them — so an odds figure can't be quietly changed later.

      
The other three buttons are the backup: copy it, email it, or paste it in Discord. Same record, handed over by hand. Nothing gets written twice, and nothing gets written until you pick one.

      
In a hurry? Just tell Ringo what you backed, the odds, how much, and who put it on. Same result, fewer taps.

03The rules

    

      

        Bet size
        
The same every time: NZ$5 a bet

        
Three bets a week is the most, between the two of us — never more after a loss. Any single bet is at most NZ$10, and we don't back anything paying less than $1.40. The NZ$5 a bet itself is under review — it's 8% of a NZ$62 pot, which is too much.

      

      

        Shortest odds we'll take
        
1.40

        
Anything shorter than 1.40 would need an advantage we won't find at TAB — its cut is a bigger slice of a short price than a long one.

      

      

        Not allowed
        
Multi bets, live betting, player bets

        
No combining bets, no betting while a game is on, no bets on individual players. There's no reliable true price to compare them against, and no way to check the number afterwards.

      

    

    
The reasoning behind these rules, and the honest limits of the whole approach, are on the method page.
